Privacy

Updated October 2, 2026. This page explains the data used by Thought Bible to provide its features.

Data you choose to provide

This can include account details, profile preferences, Bible notes and bookmarks, prayer requests, reports, and messages sent to Brother AI. Please do not include private contact details or sensitive information in public prayer requests.

How data is used

Data is used to run the app, save your selections, display community prayer content, prevent abuse, respond to reports, and provide AI-assisted replies. Some settings may be stored only on your device. When online account features are configured, account and community data may be stored in the app’s hosted database.

Service providers

Thought Bible may use service providers for authentication, database storage, Bible content, and AI responses. Information is shared with them only as needed to provide the feature you choose to use.

Community visibility and moderation

A prayer request shared with the community can be viewed by other community users. Reports are visible only to authorized moderators. Moderators may review a report and hide content that breaks the rules.

Your choices

You can avoid community features and use local app features without creating an account where available. You can also request help with account data or deletion through the support contact shown in the app’s Google Play listing.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. For privacy questions, use the support contact shown in the app’s Google Play listing.
