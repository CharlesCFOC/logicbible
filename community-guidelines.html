Prayer Community rules

Updated October 2, 2026. Prayer requests may be reviewed after a report. We may remove content or restrict access to protect the community.

Keep this space safe

  • Do not post anyone’s private information: phone number, email, home address, school, full name, account name, or location.
  • Be kind. No bullying, harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, graphic violence, spam, impersonation, or illegal content.
  • Do not ask to move a conversation to another app or contact someone privately.
  • Do not use prayer requests to give medical, legal, or emergency instructions. If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted adult.
  • Report content that may break these rules. You can also hide another author from your device.

How moderation works

Prayer requests can appear immediately on the community wall. A report is reviewed by the moderation team, which can remove content that breaks these rules. Repeated or serious violations may result in removed content or blocked community access.
